## Certification Profiles

### CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)

- **Provider:** EC-Council
- **Typical cost range:** Roughly $950-$1,500 depending on exam voucher, remote proctoring, training bundle, and region.
- **Prerequisites:** Recommended security and networking basics; formal experience or official training may be needed depending on the exam path.
- **Exam format:** Primarily multiple choice. EC-Council also offers a separate practical-oriented option in some bundles.
- **Validity:** Typically 3 years with continuing education/renewal requirements.
- **What it covers:** Broad survey of reconnaissance, enumeration, web, wireless, malware, cloud, and reporting concepts.
- **Who it is for:** Learners who need a recognized baseline cert for recruiters or internal policy requirements, especially when they are still building hands-on depth.

### OSCP (OffSec Certified Professional)

- **Provider:** Offensive Security
- **Typical cost range:** About $1,600-$2,700 depending on lab access length, Learn One-style bundles, and add-ons.
- **Prerequisites:** Comfort with Linux, TCP/IP, scripting basics, web fundamentals, and independent troubleshooting.
- **Exam format:** 24-hour practical exam plus documentation/report submission.
- **Validity:** Historically no expiration; verify current provider policy at purchase time.
- **What it covers:** Network and web exploitation, privilege escalation, pivoting, methodology, and reporting under pressure.
- **Who it is for:** Aspiring or working pentesters who want a respected proof of hands-on capability.

### OSEP (OffSec Experienced Penetration Tester)

- **Provider:** Offensive Security
- **Typical cost range:** Roughly $1,700-$3,000 depending on bundle/lab package.
- **Prerequisites:** Strong OSCP-level skills, scripting, Windows internals familiarity, and comfort operating without step-by-step guidance.
- **Exam format:** Advanced practical exam with a long-form engagement feel and reporting component.
- **Validity:** Historically no expiration.
- **What it covers:** AV/EDR evasion concepts, tooling adaptation, lateral movement, tradecraft, and advanced post-exploitation.
- **Who it is for:** Red teamers and experienced pentesters moving past entry-level network exploitation.

### OSWE (OffSec Web Expert)

- **Provider:** Offensive Security
- **Typical cost range:** Roughly $1,700-$3,000.
- **Prerequisites:** Solid web security basics, HTTP knowledge, authentication/session concepts, and ability to read source code.
- **Exam format:** Hands-on source-code review and exploitation practical with report.
- **Validity:** Historically no expiration.
- **What it covers:** White-box web assessment, code-assisted vulnerability discovery, logic flaws, and complex chaining.
- **Who it is for:** AppSec engineers, web pentesters, and consultants specializing in custom applications.

### OSED (OffSec Exploit Developer)

- **Provider:** Offensive Security
- **Typical cost range:** Roughly $1,700-$3,000.
- **Prerequisites:** Strong debugger use, Windows internals basics, memory corruption theory, and comfort with assembly-level reasoning.
- **Exam format:** Advanced practical exploit development exam with report.
- **Validity:** Historically no expiration.
- **What it covers:** Windows userland exploit development, memory corruption, mitigations, and custom exploit workflow.
- **Who it is for:** Specialists in exploit development or researchers heading toward vulnerability research.

### CompTIA Security+

- **Provider:** CompTIA
- **Typical cost range:** Usually around $400-$500 for exam-only, more with bundles.
- **Prerequisites:** Beginner-friendly; A+/Network+ level knowledge helps.
- **Exam format:** Multiple choice and performance-based questions.
- **Validity:** Usually 3 years via continuing education.
- **What it covers:** Security fundamentals, risk, access control, cryptography, architecture, incident response, and governance.
- **Who it is for:** Career changers and entry-level practitioners who need a broad security foundation.

### CompTIA PenTest+

- **Provider:** CompTIA
- **Typical cost range:** Usually around $400-$600.
- **Prerequisites:** Security+ or equivalent fundamentals; some hands-on lab experience recommended.
- **Exam format:** Multiple choice and performance-based questions.
- **Validity:** Usually 3 years via continuing education.
- **What it covers:** Scoping, rules of engagement, reconnaissance, exploitation concepts, reporting, and remediation.
- **Who it is for:** Generalist security professionals who need a balanced pentesting overview.

### CompTIA CySA+

- **Provider:** CompTIA

### GPEN

- **Provider:** GIAC / SANS

- **Prerequisites:** Solid networking, Linux/Windows basics, and exposure to pentest workflow.
- **Exam format:** Proctored multiple choice, usually open book within provider rules.
- **Validity:** Generally 4 years with renewal/CPE requirements.
- **What it covers:** Pentest process, exploitation concepts, password attacks, enumeration, and reporting.
- **Who it is for:** Professionals who want strong brand recognition and can benefit from structured SANS courseware.

## Suggested Learning Path

### Budget-conscious beginner

1. **CompTIA Security+** or **eJPT** for baseline structure.
2. Build labs with [TryHackMe](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms), [PortSwigger Academy](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms), and [OverTheWire](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms).
3. Move to **PNPT** or **OSCP** once your Linux, networking, and reporting discipline are solid.

### Web application specialist

1. Start with [PortSwigger Academy](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms) and [OWASP WSTG](./RESOURCES.md#standards--compliance).
2. Target **eWPT** or **GWAPT** for structured validation.
3. Move to **OSWE** if you want deep source-review-oriented application security credibility.

### Network / internal pentest consultant

1. Build fundamentals with **Security+**, **eJPT**, or **PenTest+**.
2. Practice AD and internal testing using [Hack The Box](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms), [TryHackMe](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms), and the tool list in [RESOURCES.md](./RESOURCES.md#tools-official-sites).
3. Target **PNPT** or **OSCP**, then **OSEP** or **GXPN** as you gain experience.

### Blue-team / DFIR crossover

1. Start with **Security+** or **CySA+**.
2. Practice on [CyberDefenders](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms) and [Blue Team Labs Online](./RESOURCES.md#practice-platforms).
3. Target **BTL1**, then **BTL2** or **GREM** depending on whether you want IR breadth or malware depth.

## How to Choose the Right Certification

- **Choose for the role, not the brand.** A consultant doing web app reviews needs different proof than a SOC analyst or malware reverser.
- **Prefer hands-on exams if you want skill proof.** OSCP, PNPT, eJPT, eCPPT, eWPT, and blue-team practicals demonstrate execution better than theory-only tests.
- **Consider hiring market geography.** CREST may matter more in the UK/EU consulting market; DoD-aligned environments may care more about CompTIA or CEH.
- **Budget matters.** SANS/GIAC content is excellent, but the price can be several times higher than self-study-first alternatives.
- **Renewal burden matters.** Expiring certs may be fine when an employer pays for CPEs; they are more painful when self-funded.
